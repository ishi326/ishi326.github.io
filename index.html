Who I am

I'm a computer science graduate from Wellesley College who cross-registered at MIT, with a background spanning AI, HCI, tangible interfaces, and interactive systems. I've explored questions ranging from how AI reasons about space to how people make decisions with AI, while building everything from physical installations to intelligent product systems. I like working at the intersection of curiosity and making — figuring out what matters, understanding how things work, and turning ideas into something people can actually interact with.
